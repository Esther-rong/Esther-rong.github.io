FAQ

Q What kind of projects do you take on?

I enjoy working on product design, front-end development, and content strategy for small teams and independent creators. If you have a project that needs a thoughtful, hands-on approach, I'd love to hear about it.

Q How can I start a collaboration with you?

The best way is to send me an email at hello@esther.design with a short description of your project and timeline. I'll get back to you within 2–3 business days.

Q Do you offer mentoring or teaching?

Yes! I run a design mentorship program and a front-end bootcamp. I also host free community workshops. Check the Programs section above for more details.

Q Are you available for freelance work?

I take on a limited number of freelance projects each quarter. Feel free to reach out — even if I'm booked, I'm happy to point you to someone great.
