About Me

Hi, I'm Esther.

I'm a student at Basis Guangzhou, and my birthday is December 19. I'm someone who likes to stay curious — whether that means learning something new in class, trying a different sport, or finding a better way to explain an idea to a friend.

Two things I really enjoy are soccer and horse riding. My favourite player is Cristiano Ronaldo. Soccer keeps me active and teaches me how important teamwork is — you can't win a match alone. Horse riding is quieter but just as rewarding: it takes patience, focus, and a real connection with the animal. Both have taught me a lot about discipline and staying calm under pressure.

At school, I like subjects that mix creativity with problem-solving. I enjoy science because it explains how things work, and I also like art and design because they let me turn ideas into something visible. Outside of class, I've taken part in clubs and activities that push me to work with others, speak up, and manage my time better.

People who know me would probably say I'm thoughtful, reliable, and a little bit curious about everything. I like helping classmates understand something they're stuck on, and I'm always happy to listen. I'm not the loudest person in the room, but I care about doing things properly and following through on what I start.

This website is my little corner of the internet. I use it to share projects I'm working on, programs and activities I've joined, and a few stories from my life. It's also a simple way for people to get in touch with me — whether it's about a school project, a collaboration, or just to say hello.

Right now, I'm still figuring out what I want to do in the future. I'm interested in design, technology, and sports, and I'd love to keep exploring how those things connect. For now, I'm taking it one step at a time: learning, practicing, and enjoying the process.

Thanks for stopping by my page. If you'd like to reach out, the contacts page has my email and a few other ways to find me. I'm always happy to meet new people and hear new ideas.
